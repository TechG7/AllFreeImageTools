How to Use:

1. Upload: Select or drag an image into the upload area.

2. Mark Object: Use your mouse or finger to paint over the unwanted object. The red selection shows the area that will be removed.

3. Brush Size: Increase or decrease the brush depending on the object size.

4. Choose Method: Smart Fill attempts to blend surrounding pixels, Smooth Fill creates a softer replacement, and Blur Fill produces a simple blurred replacement.

5. Remove: Click Remove Object to process the selected area.

6. Download: Click Download Result to save the edited image.

Helpful Tips

Get better results.

Tip 1: Paint completely over the unwanted object.

Tip 2: Include a small amount of the object's edge in the selection.

Tip 3: Use a smaller brush around detailed areas.

Tip 4: For large objects, make several smaller selections and process them separately.

Tip 5: Object removal works best when the surrounding background has similar texture or color.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can I remove a person or object?
You can mark unwanted areas and attempt to replace them using the available fill methods.

Is this cloud AI?
No. This standalone version performs the processing directly in the browser.

Will every object disappear perfectly?
No. Complex backgrounds may require multiple passes or a more advanced AI inpainting model.

Does the original image change?
No. The original file remains unchanged.
