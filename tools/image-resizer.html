About Image Resizer

Resize images directly in your browser.

Image Resizer changes the pixel dimensions of an image. It supports JPG, PNG and WEBP files and processes the image locally in your browser.

Features: Custom dimensions, aspect-ratio lock, drag & drop, quick sizes, format selection and automatic download.

Tips: Keep Ratio enabled for normal resizing. Enlarging a small image too much may reduce sharpness. PNG is useful for transparency, while JPG is commonly used for photographs.

Privacy: Image resizing is performed locally in your browser.

Can I resize PNG and WEBP?
Yes, both are supported.

Can I maintain the original ratio?
