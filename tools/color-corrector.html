Helpful Tips

Get natural-looking results.

Tip 1: Make small adjustments instead of pushing every slider to the maximum.

Tip 2: Increase saturation carefully to avoid unnatural colors.

Tip 3: Use Warmth for a warmer golden appearance and reduce it for a cooler look.

Tip 4: Use sharpness moderately to avoid harsh edges.
