Helpful Tips

Choose the right format.

JPG: Good for photographs and generally smaller than PNG.

PNG: Useful when transparency and lossless output are important.

WEBP: Often provides a good balance between quality and file size.

Tip: If converting transparent PNG to JPG, transparent areas will use a white background.

Can I control JPG/WEBP quality?
