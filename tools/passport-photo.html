Helpful Tips

For a better passport-style photo.

Tip 1: Use a clear, sharp and evenly lit photo.

Tip 2: Keep your face straight and clearly visible.

Tip 3: Avoid heavy shadows and distracting backgrounds.

Tip 4: Check the exact photo requirements of the application before printing or submitting.

Tip 5: 300 DPI is a good general setting for printing.

FAQ

Common questions.

What size is used for Indian passport photos?
The preset here is 35 × 45 mm, but individual applications can have different requirements, so verify the current official specification.

Can I make multiple copies?
Yes. Select the number of copies.

Can I enter my own size?
Yes, select Custom and enter width and height.

Does the original photo change?
No. A new image is created.

Are images uploaded?
No server upload is required.
