FAQ

Common questions.

Is this better than simple color removal?
Yes. AI attempts to identify the subject instead of only comparing pixel colors.

Does it work with people?
It is designed for common subject types including people and objects.

Does it upload my photo?
The processing is designed to run in your browser.

Why is the first conversion slower?
The AI model may need to download and initialize the first time.

Will every image be perfect?
No AI segmentation is perfect. Very complex scenes, transparent objects or heavily overlapping subjects can still require manual editing.
