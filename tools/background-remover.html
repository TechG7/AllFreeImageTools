How to Use:

1. Upload: Select or drag your image into the upload area.

2. Choose Method: Auto Background works best when the subject contrasts with the background. Corner Color is useful when the background has a similar color around the edges.

3. Adjust Tolerance: Increase it when more background needs to be removed; decrease it when parts of the subject are disappearing.

4. Edge Softness: Add a small amount to make the cutout edge smoother.

5. Download: PNG is recommended when you need a transparent background.

About Background Remover

Create transparent-background images.

Helpful Tips

Get cleaner results.

Tip 1: Plain white or solid-color backgrounds usually give the best results.

Tip 2: Start with a low tolerance and increase it gradually.

Tip 3: If the subject has similar colors to the background, automatic removal may not be perfect.

Tip 4: Use PNG to preserve transparency.

FAQ

Common questions.

Does this use a cloud AI service?
No. This standalone version processes the image in the browser.

Will every background be removed perfectly?
No. Complex backgrounds, hair and objects with colors similar to the background can require a dedicated AI segmentation model.

Why is PNG recommended?
PNG supports transparent pixels.

Does the original image change?
No. A new transparent image is created.
